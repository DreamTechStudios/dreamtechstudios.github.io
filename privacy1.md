# Smith N Smash Privacy Policy

Last updated: Oct 26, 2026

Smith N Smash does not collect, sell, or track your personal information. This policy explains the limited data that is processed so the game's multiplayer works, and how to reach me about it.

Smith N Smash ("the Game") is a virtual reality game for Meta Quest made and published by an independent solo developer ("I," "me," or "my"). This policy applies to everyone who plays the Game.

## Information We Collect

I do not directly collect or store any personal information. The Game has no accounts, sign-up forms, analytics, or advertising, and I never ask for your name, email, location, or contacts.

To run online multiplayer, the following data is processed while you play:

- **IP address** of your device, used to connect you to a multiplayer server.
- **A player or session ID**, a random identifier that tells players apart in a match.
- **Your in-game display name**, if shown to other players in your session.
- **Real-time gameplay data**, such as your position, movement, and actions in a match, sent to the other players in that session.
- **Voice audio**, only if the Game's voice chat is used, transmitted live to other players and not recorded.

This data is processed by the multiplayer service described below, not stored by me.

## Third-Party Services

The Game uses **Photon** by Exit Games GmbH to provide online multiplayer, matchmaking, and server hosting. Photon processes the IP address, player or session ID, display name, and gameplay data listed above only to connect players and run matches. Photon's handling of this data is described in [Photon's Privacy Policy](https://dashboard.photonengine.com/en-US/Account/PrivacyAndCookiePolicy).

The Game is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta's own collection of data through your headset and Meta account is covered by the [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/), not by this policy.

The Game uses no other third-party services, analytics, or advertising.

## How Information Is Used

The data above is used only to make multiplayer work:

- Connecting your device to a multiplayer server and to other players.
- Matchmaking and keeping players in the same session in sync.
- Showing your display name and actions to the other players in your match.

The data is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling, tracking you across apps, or selling to anyone.

## Sharing, Retention, and Security

**Sharing.** I do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data. The only parties that process it are Photon, to run multiplayer, and other players in your match, who see your display name and in-game actions.

**Retention.** I do not keep any of this data. Multiplayer session data exists only while you are in a match. Any limited logs Photon keeps to operate its service are retained under Photon's own policy.

**Security.** Because I do not store player data, there is no database of player information that could be exposed. Photon secures data it processes under its own security practices.

## Requesting Deletion of Your Data

You can ask for any data related to you to be deleted, or ask what data exists, at any time:

1. Email **daxonparker507@proton.me** with the subject line "Smith N Smash Data Request."
2. Include your in-game display name and, if possible, the approximate date you played, so the request can be matched.
3. I will reply within 30 days. Because I store no player data myself, I will confirm this and forward your deletion request to Photon for any data it holds about your sessions.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, or object to processing of your data. Use the same email for any of these requests.

## Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child's data has been processed, email me and I will help have it removed.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, for example because a new feature or service is added, the updated version will be posted at this same link with a new "Last updated" date.
